Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-14

This Privacy Policy explains how Tree of Moments handles information when you use the app.

Information we do not collect

Tree of Moments has no account system and uses no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, behavioral tracking, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information. There are no analytics services in the app.

Information stored on your device

Your tree parameters, growth state, sound settings, language, event preferences, and other app preferences remain in local storage on your device. The developer does not receive this data. Deleting the app removes these local settings, subject to normal device backup behavior controlled by your operating system.

Location and local weather

If you choose local weather, the app requests precise location only after asking for your permission. Your location is sent to QWeather solely to obtain weather data for your area. The developer does not retain your location. If you deny location permission, the app remains usable with manual or default weather.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments and retains purchase records under Apple's privacy policy. The app receives only the purchased product identifier and verified entitlement status. We never receive or store your name, Apple ID credentials, billing address, or payment-card details.

Children

Tree of Moments is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted publicly at github.com/adamqiu1001-cpu/tree-of-moments-legal/issues.
