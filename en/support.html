Support

Updated: 2026-10-21

Tree of Moments is a calm ambient app for iPhone: a fractal tree grows a little each time you tap, in a hand-drawn seaside scene that follows the seasons, the time of day and the weather. If something isn't working, the answers below may help. You can also write to adamqiu1001@gmail.com.

Premium and purchases

What is free, and what does Premium add?

Free: the growing tree and its scene, all three scene modes (Real Time, Random, Custom), background music, any one ambient sound at a time, the sleep timer, the breathing guide, quotes and saving pictures.

Premium adds layering several ambient sounds together, living random moments (rainbow, fireflies, shooting stars, butterflies, migrating geese, lightning, a snowman, a passing sailboat), inviting a moment into the scene from the event panel, and fine-tuning the tree's branches, leaves, flowers and colors.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, no automatic renewal and no recurring charge. The price is shown by the App Store before you confirm.

How do I restore my purchase?
  1. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy Premium.
  2. Open Tree of Moments and open its Settings.
  3. Go to Premium and tap Restore Purchases.

If nothing is found, check that you are using the same Apple Account and that you are connected to the internet, then try again.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are unable to issue refunds directly.

Scene and weather

What do the three scene modes do?
  • Real Time follows your iPhone's clock (time of day) and the current month (season). Optionally, it can also match the weather where you are.
  • Random changes the season, time of day and weather every 5 minutes.
  • Custom lets you choose the season, time of day and weather yourself.
How do I turn on Real Time weather?

Switch the scene to Real Time mode and choose to match your local weather. The first time, iOS asks for location permission; choose Allow While Using App. Precise Location can stay off: the app only needs your approximate area.

The app asks for location only when you choose this. If you don't allow it, Real Time mode still follows the time of day and the season.

How do I change or remove location permission?

Open iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Tree of Moments, then choose While Using the App or Never. You can also turn Precise Location on or off there.

The weather in the scene doesn't match the sky outside.

Local weather needs an internet connection and is refreshed from time to time rather than every minute. Apple Weather covers most regions but may occasionally be unavailable or differ from what you see outside. The scene shows five kinds of weather (sunny, cloudy, light rain, thunderstorm, snow), so similar conditions are grouped together.

Sound and sleep timer

Does the sound keep playing when my iPhone is locked?

Yes. Background music and ambient sounds continue while the screen is locked or you switch to another app, so you can relax or fall asleep with the screen off. The Ring/Silent switch does not mute it, just like a music app; use the volume buttons, or stop the sound in the app.

How does the sleep timer work?

Choose 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes, or ∞ to play without a timer. When the time is up, the sound fades out gently over about 8 seconds and stops, even if your iPhone is locked. The scene dims as well. If a phone call or an alarm interrupts the sound, the timer still ends at the time you originally set.

Why does it play together with my music or podcast?

By design, Tree of Moments mixes with other audio instead of stopping it, so you can keep rain or waves under your own music. You can lower or turn off any track, including the background music, in the sound mixer.

Why can I play only one ambient sound at a time?

In the free version, one ambient sound plays at a time alongside the background music. Layering several sounds together is part of Premium.

Pictures, language and data

How do I save a picture of my tree?

Use the save option in the app, with or without a quote on the picture. The first time, iOS asks for permission to add photos; this lets the app add pictures only, never read your library. If you declined, open iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Tree of Moments and choose Add Photos Only.

What is a seed code?

Each tree's shape comes from a seed code stored on your device. The current iPhone version does not offer seed-code import or tree restoration from a code. Keep the app installed to preserve your saved tree.

How do I switch between Chinese and English?

Use the language option in the app's Settings to switch between 简体中文 and English.

How do I delete my data?

Tree of Moments has no account and sends no data to us; your tree and settings are stored only on your iPhone. To delete them, delete the app (touch and hold its icon, then Remove App > Delete App). Your Premium purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored later. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is this app a sleep or health treatment?

No. Tree of Moments is for relaxation and enjoyment. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition, including sleep disorders. See the Terms of Use.
