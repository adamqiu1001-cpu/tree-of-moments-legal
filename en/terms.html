These Terms govern Premium purchases in Tree of Moments. Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies.

Premium products

  • Monthly: an automatically renewing monthly subscription at the localized price displayed by the App Store.
  • Annual: an automatically renewing annual subscription at the localized price displayed by the App Store.
  • Lifetime: a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks Premium for the lifetime of the app under your Apple ID.

Premium unlocks random events, detailed parameter controls, random-event preferences, and layering multiple white noises. Free features remain available without a purchase.

Billing and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm a purchase. Monthly and Annual subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current subscription period. Apple charges renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends at the applicable App Store price.

Managing subscriptions

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store account settings. Buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel an existing Monthly or Annual subscription. You must cancel the existing subscription separately if you do not want it to renew.

Restore Purchases

The app provides Restore Purchases so you can restore eligible subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase associated with your Apple ID.

Refunds and availability

Apple handles billing, cancellations, and refund requests under App Store policies. Premium access may change after a refund, revocation, subscription expiration, or other entitlement change reported by Apple.
