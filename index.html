Tree of Moments

A calm ambient app for iPhone. A fractal tree grows a little each time you tap, in a hand-drawn seaside world that follows the seasons, the time of day and the weather.

一款安静的 iPhone 氛围应用。每轻点一次，一棵分形树就生长一点；它生活在一片手绘的海湾里，随季节、时刻与天气缓缓变化。
